
# Top 10 Anomalies — FY21 Sales Data

| # | Finding | Evidence (exact figures from file) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **B2B scooter sales repeatedly priced at exact cost (~0.00% margin)** across at least 4 different accounts | SCOOTEROO Ketsanga, VSETT 10+ Electric Scooter: 53 units, Revenue **$141,441.14**, Gross Profit **$0.00**. Crooze Electric, VSETT 9+: 19 units, Revenue $29,646.15, GP $0.01. E‑OZZIE, VSETT 10+: 8 units, Revenue $21,130.91, GP $0.00. SCOOTEROO Ketsanga, VSETT 9: 47 units, Revenue $64,399.77, GP $0.03. | Combined, **>$400K of scooter revenue books ~$0 gross profit** while accessories from the *same* customers carry normal 40–85% margins. This is the single largest-dollar pattern in the data. Could be intentional (dealer floor-stock/at-cost pricing) or a costing-allocation bug — needs confirmation, not an assumption. |
| 2 | **SM Battery - Slimline / 36V 15.6AH Internal Fuse** — largest single-line loss in the retail file | 73 units, Sales ex tax **$21,962.00**, Purchase cost **$33,256.72**, Gross Profit **-$11,294.72**, margin **-51.43%** | Cost exceeds revenue by over 50% on a meaningful volume — either a supplier cost spike, a bundled-cost misallocation, or a pricing error that was never corrected. |
| 3 | **SCOOTEROO Ketsanga — VSETT 9+ Electric Scooter** | 81 units, Revenue **$120,081.93**, Gross Profit **-$1,567.36**, margin **-1.31%** | Highest-volume single scooter line trading at a net loss. |
| 4 | **Electric Kicks — VSETT 10+ Electric Scooter** | 13 units, Cost $30,917.70, Gross Profit **-$2,969.97**, margin **-10.63%**, Revenue $27,947.73 | Worst-margin high-volume B2B account line in the whole VSETT file. |
| 5 | **Litmus Lab — VSETT 9 Electric Scooter** — full write-off | 2 units, Cost of Goods **$2,998.64**, Revenue **$0.00**, Gross Profit **-$2,998.64** (100% loss) | Entire cost booked against zero revenue — looks like stock loss, demo units, or an uninvoiced shipment rather than a normal sale. |
| 6 | **Rose Bikes / Melbourne Powered account — negative margin on every scooter SKU sold** | Vsett 11+ SUPER: -$130.45 on $9,413.65; VSETT 8+: -$70.91 on $1,292.04; VSETT 9+: -$67.36 on $4,294.23 | No other B2B account shows 100% of its scooter lines negative — account-level pricing issue worth a dedicated review. |
| 7 | **"Azur side Mirror" — cost 100x the sale price** | Qty 1, Sale price $24.99, **Purchase cost $2,499.00**, Gross Profit **-$2,476.28**, margin **-10,899.99%** | Almost certainly a decimal-point data-entry error (cost should read $24.99). Worst % margin in the entire dataset, but a data-quality issue, not a real trading loss. |
| 8 | **"ZERO Helmet" — discount nearly two-thirds of sale value** | 133 units, Sales ex tax $5,705.07, **Order discount $3,743.41 (66% of sale value)**, Gross Profit **-$200.43**, margin -3.51% | Every other line's discount ratio is under ~5% of sale value; this one is a massive outlier and turns an otherwise profitable high-volume line into a loss. |
| 9 | **Corrupted product identifiers (scientific-notation barcodes)** — data integrity, ≥5 SKUs affected | `SM Display Screen C300S` barcode **"6.52E+77"**; `On Guard 8012C DT U-Lock` barcode **"7.29E+12"** (2 rows); `SM Belt Drive Kit 118T` barcode **"5.50E+98"**; `SM Tanus Wheel Protection` barcode **"5.57E+11"**; `SM Inner Tubes 27.5 X 1.9-2.1` barcode **"1.80E+04"** | Classic Excel auto-format corruption of long numeric barcodes on export. These SKUs cannot be scanned/matched against POS or supplier catalogues until fixed. |
| 10 | **Fragmented product identity — same barcode sold under two different "Item" names**, splitting true per-SKU volume | Barcode `BB642F40`: sold as "Smartmotion E20 Step Thru / Red" (161 units) **and** "SM E20 Step Thru / Red" (68 units). Barcode `6FB9B038`: "Smartmotion G3 ECity 26 250W 36V / White" (143 units) **and** "SM G3 ECity 250w 15.6AH / White 26" Tyres" (29 units) | The "Smartmotion" vs "SM" naming inconsistency recurs across a dozen+ SKUs, meaning any per-product ranking or margin analysis on the raw file **understates** true volume/performance for these items until the two names are merged. |

*Note: I excluded very small-dollar oddities (e.g. a $0.09 "Fuse" line hitting a -3,200% margin, or several $0–$1 warranty lines) from the top 10 — they are mathematically extreme but immaterial; happy to list those separately if useful.*
